III. What is processed

Delivering the site

The files of this site are delivered by Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. To deliver a page and to keep the service safe and working, Cloudflare processes what every browser sends with a request: your IP address, the time, the address asked for and the kind of browser. We keep no log files of our own and do not combine this data with any other.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR: our legitimate interest in offering the site reliably. Cloudflare works for us as a processor under its data processing addendum. It may process data in the United States; it is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More: Cloudflare's privacy policy.

What your browser stores

So that the app works, your browser stores on your device: your diagrams with their change history, the theme you chose, the workspace you had open last, and the files of the app itself so that it starts without a connection. This storage is necessary for the service you ask for (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG), so no consent is asked. You delete it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Agent sharing

If you select Start sharing, Cloudflare relays tool calls and the diagram data in their replies between your tab and the MCP client you configure. Drillgram does not save the relay messages. Your MCP client provider handles the data it receives under its own privacy policy. You can end the connection with Stop sharing.
